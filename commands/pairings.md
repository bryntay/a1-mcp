Research type in A1 Gallery for: $ARGUMENTS

- One font named: call `browse_fonts` to resolve the slug, then `get_font` for its detail
  and the sites using it.
- Two fonts named: call `find_font_pairings` with both slugs.
- A brief rather than a font ("something editorial for a law firm"): call `browse_fonts`
  filtered by classification, then show what real sites pair each with.

Report each site with its live URL and where the fonts sit — which one carries headings,
which one carries body. If the pairing appears on fewer than three sites, say so.
