Find reference in A1 Gallery for: $ARGUMENTS

Run the search, then report back:

1. Rephrase the brief in the gallery's taxonomy terms and call `search_websites`. If the
   brief names a section rather than a whole site — a hero, a pricing table — use
   `search_sections` instead.
2. Return five or six matches. For each, give the site name, its live URL, and the one
   thing that makes it a match for this brief. Not a list of its tags.
3. If the response carries `broadenedSearch`, say which terms were dropped and that the
   matches are looser than asked for.

Skip the preamble. Lead with the matches.
